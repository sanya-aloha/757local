A one-shop woodworking studio building solid hardwood furniture the old way: joined, not glued together from panels. Most hardwood comes from downed trees within an hour of the shop.

Specializes in dining tables and built-in cabinetry, with a typical lead time of six to eight weeks for a custom piece.
