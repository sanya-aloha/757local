A working farm along Back Bay growing seasonal vegetables and keeping a small flock of pasture-raised laying hens. Produce goes straight from the field to the Saturday farm stand or into weekly subscription boxes.

Farm boxes are available weekly or biweekly, sized for a family of two to four, and can be picked up at the farm or a Town Center drop point.
