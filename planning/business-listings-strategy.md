# 757 Local Business Listings & Tier Strategy - Final Brainstorm

## Overview
Two interconnected systems:
1. **PUBLIC DIRECTORY** - Consumer-facing browsable directory of all businesses (free + paid tiers)
2. **CUSTOM BUSINESS PAGES** - Paid tier businesses get their own branded listing page

Business model philosophy: **Not selling ranking or data—selling real business results** (foot traffic, sales, brand amplification, AI trust).

---

# TIER STRUCTURE & VALUE PROPOSITIONS

## TIER 1: The AI Directory
**$49/month**

A permanent, structured home base built to be read by AI search.

### What's Included:
- **Permanent directory page**, optimized to feed answers directly to ChatGPT, AI Overviews, Perplexity & Gemini
- **Verified Source Setup** so AI answer engines trust your business data on sight
- **Clean placement** in your sector's Niche Directory (Wellness, Eateries, Farmers, or Craftsmen)
- **Access to the 757 Local Perks B2B network**—trade discounts and save on other members' services

### Target Customer:
Solo entrepreneurs, freelancers, new businesses that want AI visibility without active marketing spend.

### Core Value Promise:
"Get found by AI. Join a peer network. Pay one price, get trusted everywhere."

---

## TIER 2: The Content & Broadcast Partner
**$199/month**

Everything in Tier 1, plus active digital promotion and community amplification.

### What's Included (Everything in Tier 1 PLUS):
- **Monthly Consumer Broadcast Spotlight**: A dedicated feature slot showcasing your business sent directly to our hyper-local consumer database via SMS and email newsletters
- **Community Content Amplification**: Send us your best existing Instagram Reel or TikTok once a month, and we blast it out across the official 757 Local media channels to inject our audience directly into your view count
- **"Branded Bucket" Raffle Sponsorship**: We feature your exclusive offers, gift cards, or promotional vouchers inside our physical meetup raffle bucket, putting your brand front-and-center in front of every live attendee
- **The Event Media Vault Pass**: Access to a shared library of premium, high-vibe lifestyle aesthetic B-roll footage shot exclusively at our public meetups and walks to use for your own social media text-overlays

### Target Customer:
Growing restaurants, cafes, fitness studios, service providers who want to amplify their existing content + reach engaged community.

### Core Value Promise:
"We amplify your voice. Your content reaches thousands. You get live foot traffic every month."

---

## TIER 3: The Anchor Destination Host
**$999/month**

Everything in Tier 1 & 2, plus we bring the crowd to your door.

### What's Included (Everything in Tier 1 & 2 PLUS):
- **One Dedicated Destination Event**: One massive walking club or winter cash-mob officially starts, loops, or ends inside your establishment per quarter
- **Guaranteed Cash Flood**: We require attendees to patronize the host business (buy a drink, grab a treat, sign up for a class trial) to get their raffle ticket
- **Full Editorial Coverage**: Dedicated high-end video recap of the live event posted to the resurrected 757 Local social channels

### Target Customer:
Established businesses, destinations, anchors that want to be the featured host and draw guaranteed foot traffic + sales.

### Core Value Promise:
"We bring the crowd to your door. Guaranteed. Every quarter, your business is the destination."

# PUBLIC DIRECTORY PAGE STRUCTURE

## Purpose
End consumers discover and browse local businesses across 4 Priority Niches.

## Category Navigation
- **4 Primary Niches**:
  1. Wellness & Boutique Fitness
  2. Local Eateries & Cafes
  3. Small Farmers & Growers
  4. Independent Craftsmen & Makers

- **City Filter**: Virginia Beach | Norfolk | Chesapeake | Suffolk | Portsmouth | Hampton | Newport News

## Business Listing Card Design

### Free Tier Card (De-emphasized)
```
[Business Name]
Category Badge
City
Star Rating (if applicable)
"Basic Listing" label
Quick actions: Website | Phone
```
- Position: Lower in search results
- Click: Links to website or contact info only (no custom 757 page)

### Tier 1 Card ($49) - "AI Directory"
```
[Business Logo/Image]
Business Name
Category Badge | City
⭐ VERIFIED SOURCE (AI badge)
Star Rating
Quick Info: Hours | Phone | Website
"View Profile" button → Links to Custom Page
```
- Higher ranking in search results (2nd tier)
- AI trust badge visible
- Links to custom business page

### Tier 2 Card ($199) - "Broadcast Partner"
```
[Business Logo/Image]
Business Name
Category Badge | City
⭐⭐ FEATURED (stronger AI + broadcast badge)
Star Rating
Quick Info: Hours | Phone | Website
Latest Broadcast Feature (link to recent newsletter spotlight)
Photo carousel (3-5 images)
"View Profile" button → Links to Custom Page
```
- Higher ranking (1st tier, above Tier 1)
- Larger card with more visual real estate
- Shows recent broadcast/amplification activity

### Tier 3 Card ($999) - "Destination Host"
```
[Business Logo/Image]
Business Name
Category Badge | City
⭐⭐⭐ ANCHOR DESTINATION (premium badge)
Star Rating
"Next Destination Event: [Date]" callout
Quick Info: Hours | Phone | Website
Photo carousel (6+ images)
"View Profile & Upcoming Events" button → Links to Custom Page
```
- **ALWAYS appears first** in search results
- Largest card size
- Prominent upcoming event visibility
- Premium visual treatment

## Search & Filter Features
- **Search bar** (full-text search)
- **Category tabs** (switch between 4 niches)
- **City multi-select** (filter by one or more cities)
- **Sorting options**:
  - Relevance (default)
  - Highest rated
  - Featured (shows Tier 2 & 3 first)
  - Upcoming Events (Tier 3 events first)

# CONTENT MANAGEMENT & ONBOARDING

## Submission Process (All Tiers)
1. Business completes application form (via partner page)
2. Uploads photos, business details
3. Selects tier
4. **Curator reviews** (fact verification, photo quality, spam check)
5. Approved = Goes live on directory + custom page
6. Rejected = Feedback provided, business can resubmit

## Curation Standards
- Verify business legitimacy & contact info
- Check photo quality (clear, professional, not misleading)
- Verify business description for accuracy
- Ensure compliance with 757 Local values (community-focused, authentic)

# Key Strategic Insights

1. **Value is Real & Tangible**: Not abstract ranking/visibility—it's broadcast reach, foot traffic, media production, and AI trust
2. **Each tier has clear ROI**: Tier 1 = trust, Tier 2 = amplification, Tier 3 = guaranteed traffic + sales
3. **Tier 3 is the Crown Jewel**: One destination event per quarter with mandatory patronage is a strong, repeatable revenue driver
4. **Free tier serves funnel**: Gets businesses in the door, positions paid tiers as obvious upgrade
5. **Community-centric**: B2B network + raffle bucket + events = strong community stickiness, not just transactional
