Every piece starts on the wheel in a small studio a few blocks from the Oceanfront. Stoneware is fired twice, glazed by hand, and finished in batches small enough that no two plates are quite the same.

Best known for a warm, sand-toned dinnerware line carried by a handful of restaurants across Hampton Roads, and for taking on custom commissions for weddings and housewarmings.
